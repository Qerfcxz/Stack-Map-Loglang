1. Linguistic Philosophy: The Open Relational "Annotative Model"
================================================================================

In mainstream formal semantics (e.g., Montague Grammar) and conventional loglang designs,
there is a pervasive tendency to hardcode concrete metaphysical or physical models into
the grammatical core—such as prescribing fixed Kripkean possible-world sets W, absolute
Newtonian spatiotemporal coordinates, or elaborate higher-order type hierarchies. This
practice inflates type systems and robs the language of flexibility across diverse
physical frameworks or pragmatic contexts.

SML adopts a "metaphysically agnostic", open topological approach, treating semantic
generation as an iterative "progressive annotation process" over concepts and events:

1.1 The Grammar Engine Only Guarantees Connectivity and Closure
    The core abstract machine of SML enforces only two fundamental mathematical laws:
    - The topological conservation law of binary modification: every relation R consumes
      exactly one entity S (ΔR = ΔS);
    - The structural rigor of generalized quantifier closure: the orthogonal partitioning
      between restrictor (Imap) and nuclear scope (Omap).
    Whatever worldviews, temporal topologies, or physical models a proposition conveys are
    delegated to specific lexical relations; the grammar kernel remains strictly neutral.

1.2 Relational Dimensionality Reduction of Linguistic Categories
    By appending binary relational edges (S R) to core entities or events, diverse classical
    linguistic categories are uniformly reduced to local annotations:
    - Tense and Aspect: Rather than introducing specialized tense syntax, these are modeled
      as spatiotemporal coordinates attached to the event variable e (e.g., Occurred_At(e, Yesterday),
      Frame_Of_Reference(e, Earth_Clock)). This allows the language to express coarse everyday
      temporality as easily as relativistic spacetime.
    - Modality and Probability: Avoids hardcoding possible-world semantics by converting modality
      into graded degrees and evidential annotations (e.g., Probability(e, 0.5), Evidential_Source(e, Direct_Sight)).
    - Question Interface: Interrogatives are modeled as unclosed placeholder variables (Named Holes).
      In dialogue, the respondent explicitly declares which named hole is instantiated, enabling
      deterministic, ambiguity-free pragmatic exchange.
    - Plurality and Mereology: Avoids complex individual algebras by utilizing unary set operations.
      Transforming set S into a singleton or power set allows the set topology itself to accommodate
      collective vs. distributive readings.

1.3 From Tree Modifications to Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAG)
    Concatenative syntax natively constructs nested modification trees of arbitrary depth
    (in the form of "S S R S S R R R"). Combined with cross-clause environment mapping
    (Upload / Download), cross-references can be established between distinct tree branches,
    losslessly generating arbitrary Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) and eliminating expressive
    blind spots common in complex natural language structures.

2. Word Formation and Concept Generation: Compound Template System
================================================================================

As a constructed language, SML abandons traditional morphological derivation based on
complex affixation or inflection, adopting "compound templates" (macros) to fulfill the
role of hardcoded lexical roots.

2.1 The Nature of Compound Templates
    Compound templates are, at their core, SML macros. Existing adjective macros are basic
    compound templates (e.g., Tall_Macro takes entity x and yields a restrictive formula over
    a height scale). In vocabulary expansion, compound templates handle the full scope of
    derivational morphology:
    - For example, constructing "fisherman / one who processes fish": instead of introducing
      specialized occupational affixes (-er, -ist), a compound template "x whose occupation is
      processing y" is defined. Supplying "Person:S" and "Fish:S" automatically assembles the
      composite concept via underlying relations.

2.2 Coexistence of Precise and Fuzzy Relations
    - Precise Composition: In academic or legal contexts requiring strict precision, templates
      explicitly specify fine-grained relations (e.g., "Occupation_Is", "Processes").
    - Fuzzy Relations (Generic Association): In everyday communication, excessive precision leads
      to verbose utterances. The system permits a generic "Associated_With(A, B)" relation, stating
      merely that concept A and B are related, leaving pragmatic disambiguation to context (akin
      to natural noun-noun compounds, e.g., "fish knife" meaning either a knife for fish or a
      knife shaped like a fish).
    This mechanism allows the language to slide seamlessly between absolute logical rigor and
    economical everyday communication.

2.3 Application of Degree Semantics
    For gradable adjectives and adverbs, compound templates converge on normalized scale intervals:
    - Adjectives function as binary relations over measurement sets (e.g., normalized interval 0~1),
      such as "Weight_Measure:R";
    - Adverbs function as contraction operators or quantifiers directly targeting that measurement
      interval (e.g., Forall_Adv_Macro maps to universal quantification over the interval, while
      Local_Neg_Macro inverts polarity);
    - This eliminates the need for deeply nested fictive events to express "very", "slightly", or
      "heavy as an elephant", keeping formulas compact.

3. Natural Language Conception: Phonology, Orthography, and Pure Isolating Typology
================================================================================

To transition this abstract logic deduction engine into a speakable, readable, and writable
human language, the surface linguistic structure incorporates the following designs:

3.1 Minimal Phonology
    - Minimal Distinction Burden: Confusing phonemic contrasts are eliminated, including
      voiced/voiceless, aspirated/unaspirated, and pitch/stress oppositions. The phonemic
      inventory resembles a minimal phonetic system akin to standard Hiragana.
    - Strict Syllable Patterns and Pauseless Tokenization:
      Words strictly follow an alternating vowel-consonant structure (e.g., standard VCVC...V).
      Because word boundaries exhibit strict self-delimiting phonotactic properties, listeners
      segment words in continuous speech without relying on inter-word pauses, eliminating
      segmentation ambiguity.

3.2 Orthography Combining Alphabetic and Abugida Characteristics
    - A custom writing system blending linear alphabetic features with abugida aesthetics;
    - Consonants serve as base glyphs, while vowels attach as dependent diacritics or ligatures,
      ensuring high information density, aesthetic balance, and strict one-to-one logical phonetic rules.

3.3 Pure Isolating / Analytic Typology
    - Zero morphological inflection: no grammatical case, no verb conjugation, no tense forms;
    - Functions traditionally borne by inflection are decomposed into independent syntactic
      particles or compound templates;
    - Word roots remain morphologically immutable; semantics is entirely dictated by topological
      consumption order in the concatenative state machine.

4. Architecture Layering: The Two-Pass Pipeline Conception
================================================================================

In designing a pure stack-based abstract machine, stack-effect avalanches pose an engineering
risk: minor rule adjustments can cascade unpredictably. To maintain tractability, the
architecture adopts a decoupled "Two-Pass" pipeline:

4.1 Pass 1: Surface & Pragmatics Preprocessor
    Handles dimensions beyond core truth-conditional (at-issue) semantics:
    - Speaker attitude and mood (epistemic particles, evidential tags);
    - Non-restrictive modifications (parentheticals/appositives that do not affect the main
      truth value, serving as background assertions);
    - Desugaring of macros and syntactic sugar into compliant, raw SML instruction streams.

4.2 Pass 2: SML Semantic Kernel
    Focuses exclusively on first-order formal truth-conditional computation:
    - Strictly executes state transitions across the 6 Stacks, 1 Stream, 1 Register, and 8 Maps;
    - Manages thematic assembly, Generalized Quantifier (GQ) closures, donkey anaphora, and scope resolution.
    This division prevents pragmatic complexities from turning the core engine into a "Turing Tar-Pit",
    while maximizing the power of linear quantifier closures.

5. Conclusion: Balance Between Microkernel and Open Ecosystem
================================================================================

The transition of SML from empirical experimentation to formal specification marks a breakthrough:
achieving a theoretical closed loop uniting "Noun-Verb Parity", "Relational Annotation", and
"Generalized Quantifier Duality".

For the formal kernel, the rule set should undergo a "Feature Freeze" upon completing the
permutation pipeline (PR stack) and degree semantic specifications:
- No domain-specific ad-hoc instructions should be injected into the core state machine;
- The core microkernel must remain cold, pure, and deterministic;
- Expressive power is left to the open vocabulary (S and R sets) and compound template ecosystem.

This discipline curbs engineering complexity and establishes SML as a universal, unbiased,
foundational semantic logic protocol.
