1. LINGUISTIC PHILOSOPHY: THE OPEN RELATIONAL "ANNOTATION MODEL"
================================================================================

In mainstream formal semantics (e.g., Montague Grammar) and several logical language
designs, there is a tendency to hardcode specific metaphysical or physical assumptions
directly into the grammar. Examples include baking in a fixed Kripkean possible-world
universe W, an absolute Newtonian temporal topology, or rigid higher-order type
hierarchies. This often leads to severe type explosion and a lack of resilience
across differing physical paradigms or pragmatic contexts.

SML chooses an alternative path grounded in "metaphysical agnosticism" and open
topological graph construction: treating semantic composition as a process of
progressive relational annotation.

1.1 The Engine Enforces Connectivity, Not Metaphysics
    The SML abstract machine enforces only two algebraic invariants:
    - Relational Conservation: Every binary relation R strictly consumes one
      entity S (ΔR = ΔS).
    - Generalized Quantifier Scoping: The orthogonal separation of Restrictor (Imap)
      and Nuclear Scope (Omap).
    The engine remains agnostic regarding worldview, temporal structure, or modality;
    these domain semantics belong entirely to the vocabulary of relations.

1.2 Linguistic Phenomena as Relational Annotations
    By iteratively attaching binary relations (S R) to core entities or events,
    classical linguistic phenomena reduce to localized annotations:
    - Tense and Aspect: Rather than introducing specialized grammatical categories,
      tense is represented as spatiotemporal coordinates attached to the event
      entity e (e.g., "Occurred_At(e, Yesterday)", "Frame_Of_Reference(e, Earth_Clock)").
      This cleanly accommodates both everyday coarse temporal descriptions and
      relativistic spacetime frames without grammatical changes.
    - Modality and Probability: Avoids hardcoding possible-world accessibility
      relations. Modality is expressed through graded attribution and epistemic
      evidence (e.g., "Probability(e, 0.5)", "Evidence_Source(e, Direct_Sight)").
    - Question Interface: Wh-questions are modeled as named placeholder variables
      (Holes). Respondents explicitly specify which hole is being filled, yielding
      deterministic and unambiguous information exchange.
    - Plurality and Mereology: Avoids complex individual sum algebras by operating
      directly on base sets (transforming category S into singletons or power sets),
      accommodating collective and distributive readings via set operations.

1.3 From Trees to Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs)
    Concatenative postfix scheduling naturally generates arbitrarily deep nested
    trees (e.g., "S S R S S R R R"). Combined with cross-clause environment mappings
    (Upload / Download), different branches can establish cross-scope coreferences,
    allowing the system to express arbitrary Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) with zero
    syntactic leakage.

2. WORD FORMATION & CONCEPT GENERATION: COMPOUND TEMPLATES
================================================================================

As a speakable logical language, SML discards traditional inflectional morphology
and rigid affixation tables, adopting "Compound Templates" (Macros) to replace
hardcoded derivational roots.

2.1 The Nature of Compound Templates
    Compound templates are, at their core, SML macros. Existing adjective macros
    represent the simplest category of compound templates (e.g., "Tall_Macro" takes
    entity x and generates a formula restricting x by a height scale).
    In lexical expansion, compound templates handle all derivational morphology:
    - Example: Constructing "Fisherman / Person who processes fish": Rather than
      inventing dedicated agentive affixes (like -er or -ist), one defines a compound
      template "x whose occupation is processing y". Supplying "Person:S" and "Fish:S"
      assembles the target concept via clear underlying relational edges.

2.2 Precise vs. Vague Compounding
    - Precise Compounding: In academic, legal, or technical contexts, templates
      specify explicit relational predicates (e.g., "Occupation_Is", "Processes").
    - Vague Relations (Generic Association): In natural everyday conversation,
      excessive precision can lead to verbosity. SML permits a generic relation
      "Associated_With(A, B)", merely asserting an underspecified connection between
      A and B. The specific pragmatic interpretation is left to context (mirroring
      natural language compounds like "fish knife", which could be a knife for fish
      or a knife shaped like a fish).
    This design enables smooth code-switching between formal logic and practical dialogue.

2.3 Application in Scale Semantics
    For gradable adjectives and adverbs, compound templates converge cleanly into
    normalized scale intervals:
    - Adjectives function as binary relations over a normalized scale set (such as 0~1);
    - Adverbs function as quantifier transformations or scale operators over that
      interval (e.g., "Forall_Adverb_Macro" universally quantifies over the scale);
    - Modifiers like "very", "slightly", or "heavy as an elephant" avoid complex
      nested dummy events, keeping output logical formulas compact.

3. NATURAL LANGUAGE ENGINEERING: PHONOLOGY, ORTHOGRAPHY, AND ISOLATING TYPOLOGY
================================================================================

To evolve the abstract reduction engine into a speakable, auditory-friendly human
language, the surface linguistic structure is designed according to three tenets:

3.1 Minimal Phonology
    - Low Perceptual Burden: Eliminates easily confusable phonemic contrasts,
      including voicing contrasts (p/b, t/d), aspiration contrasts, and lexical
      stress/pitch distinctions.
    - Alternating Syllables and Pause-Free Word Segmentation:
      Words strictly follow an alternating vowel-consonant structure (e.g., standard
      VCVC...V patterns). Because word boundaries are phonotactically self-delimiting,
      listeners can tokenize continuous speech without phonetic pauses, preventing
      word-segmentation ambiguities.

3.2 Orthography Combining Alphabetic and Abugida Features
    - Employs a custom script balancing linear alphabetic properties with the compact
      structural beauty of an abugida;
    - Consonants serve as base glyphs, while vowels attach as diacritics or ligature
      strokes, ensuring high visual density and unambiguous one-to-one grapheme-to-phoneme
      correspondence.

3.3 Purely Isolating / Analytic Typology
    - Zero inflectional morphology: No grammatical cases, no verb conjugations, no
      grammatical gender, and no synthetic tense markers;
    - All grammatical functions are deconstructed into standalone lexical tokens
      or compound templates;
    - Word forms remain invariant; semantic scope is determined purely by concatenative
      topological reduction order.

4. ARCHITECTURAL STRATIFICATION: THE TWO-PASS PIPELINE CONCEPTION
================================================================================

In designing a purely concatenative abstract machine, an unavoidable engineering
reality is that stack invariants are fragile: slight adjustments to low-level
rules can cause cascading regressions ("stack effect cascades"). To minimize
mental overhead, future implementations plan to adopt a decoupled "Two-Pass" pipeline:

4.1 Pass 1: Surface & Pragmatics Preprocessor
    Handles non-at-issue pragmatic dimensions:
    - Speaker attitudes and evidential particles;
    - Non-restrictive modifiers (e.g., appositives and parentheticals that do not
      alter the core truth conditions of the matrix proposition);
    - Macro unpacking and syntactic desugaring into canonical SML instruction streams.

4.2 Pass 2: SML Core Semantic Kernel
    Focuses exclusively on first-order truth-conditional deductions:
    - Executes deterministic state transitions over the 6 Stacks, 1 Stream,
      1 Register, and 8 Maps;
    - Resolves thematic role bindings, Generalized Quantifier closures (GQ),
      donkey sentence anaphora, and scope demarcations.
    This separation prevents pragmatic noise from polluting the core machine,
    avoiding the "Turing Tarpit" while retaining linear reduction efficiency.

5. CONCLUSION: BALANCING THE MICROKERNEL AND AN OPEN ECOSYSTEM
================================================================================

SML's evolution from early heuristics (prior to v0.99) to v1.0 achieved its critical
breakthrough by unifying noun-verb parity, relational annotations, and the dual-track
restrictor/nuclear scope model.

For the formal core of this language, the rule set should undergo a strict
"Feature Freeze" once the permutation pipeline (PR stack) and scale semantics are
established:
- Avoid introducing domain-specific opcodes into the machine kernel;
- Maintain the microkernel as deterministic, minimal, and algebraically clean;
- Delegate expressive power to an open vocabulary of relations (S and R) and
  the compound template ecosystem.

This discipline not only controls personal engineering complexity, but also secures
SML's theoretical role as a neutral, universal semantic foundation.
